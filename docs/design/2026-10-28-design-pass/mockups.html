Work

  1. No app navigation and no top bar. The page title and "Choose areas" are loose in the content, and areas only exist as one long scroll with no way to jump between them.
  2. The card header spreads over the full width. The title sits at the far left and its progress, "Show done" switch and an unlabelled checkbox (which checks off the whole project and its tasks) sit 1,000 px away at the far right. At 1280 px nothing caps the line length.
  3. Every task row shows every control all the time: a boxed date field (an empty one still reads "mm/dd/yyyy"), "Clear due date", a priority "⋯" that doesn't show the priority, and "Open in Tasks". The task title is the quietest thing in its own row.
  4. Done items fade by opacity, twice. The class and an inline style both apply 0.6, so a done subtask under a done parent drops to about 0.36: unreadable and below contrast minimums.
  5. Boxed "+ Add task" and "+ New project" fields on every card add a heavy outlined box per project, so an area of eight projects carries eight empty inputs.
  6. Someday cards fade the whole card to 0.6, including the controls, so they look disabled rather than parked. Empty projects show "0/0" and a bar.
  7. Inconsistent glyphs and order: text triangles (▶ ▼) instead of the Nextcloud icon set, and the Done strip sits above "+ New project", so new projects appear to be filed under Done.
  8. Not visible here: the Needs filing tray is built but not mounted; there is no "move to project" control (drag groups are per parent, so a task can't be dragged to another project); the phone layout is a long list of 44 px overrides, so rows wrap to three lines; the first-run state repeats "Choose your areas" as a paragraph and a button; the settings dialog has no Cancel.

  • Navigation is a table of contents. "All areas" keeps today's one-page panel (spec M1). Clicking an area scrolls to it rather than hiding the others. Needs filing gets its own item with a count. "Report a bug" (M21) and "Choose areas" sit in the footer.
  • The top bar holds one action. "New project" asks which area when used from the top bar. Each area also keeps its quiet "New project in Home" row. Collapse all is an icon button.
  • Projects are bands, not boxed cards. The project checkbox sits where task checkboxes sit, the title reads first, and progress becomes a short bar with "3 of 7" right beside it. A collapsed project shows its next due date. Collapsing is remembered per project in this browser, like Show done.
  • Task rows are just text. The due date appears only when one is set (red when overdue). Priority is the checkbox outline colour, as in the Tasks app (high red, medium amber, low blue). The drag grip and "⋯" menu appear on hover. The menu holds Due date, Priority, Move to project and Open in Tasks (see d).
  • The reading column stops at 920 px, so a title is never 1,000 px from its own progress bar. A status line at the bottom gives the totals and when the data was last refreshed (M13).
  • The conflict notice (M14) stays a Nextcloud toast. The reloaded row is also tinted for a few seconds, so you can see which task changed.

  • The project "⋯" menu repeats the header controls in words: Show done, Check off project, Open in Tasks. "Mark blocked" and "Move to Someday" are new and optional: they would add or remove the blocked and someday categories. Cut them if you'd rather keep 1.0 to its Musts.

  • The tray is a page, not a box tucked under the areas. It's reached from the navigation, where the count is highlighted while anything is waiting. Each item says why it's there and which list it's on.
  • One move flow, two ways in. The dialog lists projects by area, with search. It says what will happen before you confirm.
